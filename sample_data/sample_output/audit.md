- **robots.txt allows AI crawlers** (10 pts): robots.txt must not block GPTBot / CCBot / Google-Extended.
- **FAQ content or FAQPage schema** (20 pts): Questions + answers in the page help AI engines quote it.
- **Structured data (JSON-LD) present** (15 pts): Schema.org markup (WebSite, FAQPage, HowTo, Article...) helps citation.
- **Clear heading structure (H1 + H2s)** (10 pts): One H1 and descriptive H2s make the page easy to summarise.
- **Dates / freshness signals** (10 pts): A published/updated date tells engines the facts are current.
- **Substantive content (>= 300 words)** (10 pts): Thin pages rarely get cited; 300+ words is the floor here.
- **Descriptive internal links** (5 pts): Named links (not 'click here') help engines follow the site.
